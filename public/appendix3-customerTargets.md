# Appendix 3: Target Customer Intelligence Report
## Deep Research on Top AML Transaction Monitoring Prospects by Market

**Research Date:** January 2026
**Scope:** Detailed intelligence on Tier 3-4 banking institutions across primary target markets
**Methodology:** Web-based research of publicly available information including regulatory filings, press releases, news articles, and corporate disclosures

---

## Executive Summary

This appendix provides detailed intelligence on the highest-priority target customers for AML Transaction Monitoring solutions across the four primary geographic markets identified in the main report: United States, United Kingdom, Germany, and Canada.

The research identifies institutions with the following characteristics that make them high-value prospects:
- Asset sizes in the USD 500M to USD 50B range (Tier 3-4)
- Recent regulatory challenges or compliance investments indicating awareness of AML deficiencies
- Known technology transformation initiatives suggesting openness to new vendors
- Publicly disclosed compliance leadership structures enabling targeted outreach
- Evidence of current vendor relationships nearing potential renewal cycles

**Key Findings:**
- Multiple UK institutions have faced significant AML-related fines in 2024-2025, creating urgent demand for improved systems
- US regional banks are investing heavily in compliance infrastructure ahead of the USD 100B asset threshold
- Technology vendor consolidation is creating opportunities for new entrants
- NICE Actimize, SymphonyAI NetReveal, and Verafin dominate current market but face renewal cycles

### 9.2 Limitations and Caveats

**Information Availability:**
- Many banks do not publicly disclose current AML technology vendors (competitive sensitivity)
- Contract renewal dates are rarely public information (estimated based on implementation dates + typical contract lengths)
- Chief Compliance Officer and MLRO identities not always prominently featured in public materials
- Budget figures and technology spending plans are confidential

**Research Completeness:**
- Some target institutions could not be fully researched due to web search unavailability during research period
- German market research particularly limited by language barriers and data localisation
- Canadian Schedule II bank research incomplete

**Estimates and Assumptions:**
- Asset figures estimated where 2025 data not yet published (based on latest available reports)
- Pricing recommendations based on tier definitions from main report
- Contract renewal timing estimated based on typical 3-5 year SaaS contract lengths
- FDE service need estimated based on bank size and location (may vary by institution)

**Time Sensitivity:**
- All information accurate as of January 2026 research date
- Regulatory fines, executive appointments, and M&A activity change rapidly
- Vendor relationships may have changed since latest public disclosure
- Recommend quarterly refresh of target account intelligence
